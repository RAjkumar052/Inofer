Infoer

Pick photos, videos, PDFs or Office files. Infoer reads the hidden data inside them: which phone or camera made them, when, where (GPS), and what software touched them.

Showing an example result. Select your own files to replace it.

WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram delete the original phone model and GPS location before sending, so no app can bring them back. Infoer still shows what survives: which app it came from, the date it was received, how much it was compressed, and hidden app tags. To keep everything, the sender must share the photo as a "Document" in WhatsApp, or send the original file another way.
